Privacy Policy

Quran with Pashto and Dari Translation (د قرآن کریم پښتو او دري ترجمه) · App ID: com.stoone.quran · Last updated: 15 October 2026

Summary

The app does not collect, store on any server, sell, or share your personal data. It has no accounts, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking.

Data stored on your device

  • Bookmarks, your last-read position, and settings (language, theme, font size, prayer-time preferences) are saved only on your phone. They are deleted when you uninstall the app.

Location

  • If you allow it, the app uses your device location to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. This calculation happens on your phone; your location is not sent to us.
  • To show the name of your city, or when you search for a city by name, the app uses your phone's built-in geocoding service (Google on Android, Apple on iPhone). Those services may receive the coordinates or the text you search for, under their own privacy policies.
  • Location is optional. You can deny it and choose your city by searching instead.

Notifications

Prayer-time reminders are scheduled locally on your phone. No notification data is sent to us.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
